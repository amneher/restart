# Frequently Asked Questions

---

**What is a divorce registry?**
A divorce registry is a gift registry for people rebuilding their lives after divorce. It works the same way a wedding or baby registry does: you make a list of things you need, share it, and the people who care about you buy gifts directly from the retailer. The reStart is a free platform built specifically for divorce registries, and you can add items from any online retailer.

---

**What is The reStart?**
The reStart is a free divorce registry platform for people rebuilding their lives after divorce or separation. You create a registry, add items from any online retailer, and share it with friends and family so they can help you set up your new home. Whether you need a new set of sheets, a toaster, or a couch to call your own, The reStart makes it easy for the people who love you to help you start fresh.

---

**Is The reStart free?**
Yes, The reStart is free to use. Join, read some articles, create a registry with the items you need from the retailers you like. Some retailers may pay us a referral commission, but that doesn't affect the price gift-givers pay.

---

**Is a divorce registry appropriate?**
Yes, a divorce registry is appropriate, because divorce often means rebuilding an entire household from scratch. A registry gives friends and family a clear, practical way to help. Most people genuinely want to do something; a registry just tells them what. If a wedding or a new baby warrants a registry, a divorce does too.

---

**What do you put on a divorce registry?**
A divorce registry usually holds the household basics you need to make a new place feel like home: bedding, towels, cookware, a coffee maker, small appliances, and furniture. A good place to start is whatever you shared with your former partner and no longer have. You can also add things that feel more like a fresh start than a replacement: a nice set of knives, a piece of art, a good lamp. There's no rulebook. Add what you actually need.

---

**Do I need to be legally divorced to use The reStart?**
No, you don't need to be legally divorced to use The reStart. It's for anyone at any point in the process, whether you're just starting to think about it, in the middle of a separation, or already on the other side. You don't need a court date, a lawyer, or a final decree.

---

**Can I create a divorce registry for someone else?**
Yes, you can create a registry on behalf of someone you love who is going through a divorce. Give it their name, add the things you think they need, and share it with the people in their life. It's a practical way to organize the support that's already there.

---

**How do I create a divorce registry on The reStart?**
To create a divorce registry, sign up for a free account and go to [Start a Registry](/start-a-registry/). Give your registry a name and a short description (a few words about yourself, or about the person you're building it for), then start adding items from any retailer you like. Setup takes about five minutes.

---

**How do I add items to my registry?**
To add an item, paste a link to any product from any online retailer into your registry dashboard. The reStart pulls in the product name, image, and price automatically. You can also add a note telling gift-givers why you want it or what it's replacing.

---

**How do friends and family buy gifts from my registry?**
Gift-givers buy directly from the retailer: clicking an item on your registry takes them to the retailer's website to complete the purchase. The reStart doesn't handle payments; the transaction is entirely between the gift-giver and the retailer. After buying, they mark the item as purchased on your registry so others know it's taken care of.

---

**Who can see my registry?**
New registries are private: only you and the people you invite by email or username can see them, and invitees sign in to view. You can make a registry public at any time from its settings. A public registry can be viewed by anyone with the link and appears on the [Find a Registry](/find-a-registry/) page.

---

**How do I share my registry?**
To share a public registry, copy its link from your dashboard and send it however you like: text, email, a group chat, or a note in a card. To share a private registry, invite people by email or username from your registry settings. You don't have to announce it any particular way; share it when and how it feels right.

---

**What if an item is marked purchased but never arrives?**
Delivery problems are handled between the gift-giver and the retailer, because The reStart isn't part of the transaction. We recommend gift-givers keep their order confirmation from the retailer in case something goes wrong.

---

**Can I have more than one registry?**
Yes, you can create as many registries as you need: one for your new apartment, one for the home office you're finally setting up, whatever fits.

---

**Can I edit my registry after it's published?**
Yes, you can add, edit, and remove items at any time, and changes show up immediately.

---

**How do I delete my account?**
You can delete your account yourself from the My Account page by choosing **Delete account**. This permanently removes your account and all associated data, and you'll receive one final confirmation email.
