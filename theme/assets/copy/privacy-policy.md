# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: May 3, 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains how The Restart Co ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use reStart at the-restart.co (the "Site"). By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

**Information you provide directly:**
- Account information: display name, email address, and password when you register
- Registry information: registry title, story, event type, event date, and items you add
- Communications: messages you send us via the contact form

**Information collected automatically:**
- Usage data: pages visited, links clicked, and time spent on the Site
- Device and browser information: IP address, browser type, and operating system
- Cookies and similar technologies (see Section 5)

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Create and maintain your account
- Display and share your registry with people you invite
- Send transactional emails (purchase notifications, password resets)
- Respond to your support requests and inquiries
- Improve and maintain the Site
- Comply with legal obligations

We do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling.

---

## 3. Registry Privacy

Registries you create are **private by default** — only people you explicitly invite can view them. If you set your registry to public, your registry title and items will be visible to anyone with a link.

Your email address is never displayed publicly on the Site.

---

## 4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only in the following circumstances:

- **Service providers:** Third-party vendors who help us operate the Site (hosting, email delivery, analytics) under strict data processing agreements
- **Retailers:** When you add items from third-party retailers (Amazon, Etsy, etc.), those links may include affiliate tracking parameters. We do not share your personal information with retailers directly
- **Legal requirements:** If required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the public
- **Business transfers:** In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case we will notify you before your information is transferred

---

## 5. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you logged in, remember your preferences, and understand how the Site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though disabling them may affect Site functionality.

We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

---

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by law.

Registry data (items, event details) is deleted when you delete your registry or your account.

---

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- Access the personal information we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate information
- Request deletion of your information
- Object to or restrict certain uses of your information
- Receive a copy of your information in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@the-restart.co. We will respond within 30 days.

---

## 8. Children's Privacy

The Site is intended for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

The Site contains links to third-party retailer websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies before making any purchases.
